### 6. Privacy & Telemetry Statement

OnSet Gallery operates strictly offline:
- **Zero Telemetry:** The Software does not contain tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising beacons, or background telemetry services.
- **Local Processing:** All image decoding, thumbnail caching, and local pre-cull duplicate evaluations occur on your local device.
- **No Remote Transmission:** Your photos, file paths, project names, and metadata are never transmitted to remote servers, external APIs, or third parties.
